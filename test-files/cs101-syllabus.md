### Academic Integrity Policy
All homework must be your own work. You may discuss high-level approaches with classmates, but **sharing code or copying solutions is strictly prohibited**. First offense results in a zero on the assignment; second offense is reported to the Dean of Students. AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) may **not** be used on homework or exams but are allowed for Project 2 with proper attribution.

### Exam Policies
- **Midterm:** Closed-book, one handwritten cheat sheet (letter size, both sides). Covers Weeks 1–7.
- **Final:** Cumulative, closed-book, two handwritten cheat sheets allowed. Held in **Lecture Hall 300** (not our regular classroom).
- No electronic devices except a basic scientific calculator (no graphing calculators).
- Make-up exams only with documented medical/family emergency — must notify instructor within 24 hours.

### Regrade Policy
Regrade requests must be submitted in writing within **7 days** of receiving the graded assignment. Include a clear explanation of why you believe the grade is incorrect. Submit regrade requests through Gradescope, not email. Regrades may result in a higher or lower score.
